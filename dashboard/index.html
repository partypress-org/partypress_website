Before you analyse the data

  • Compare topics with policy-conditioned shares (as in the chart above). How much non-policy content a release has is the least certain quantity in the data.
  • Compare rather than read levels. Differences across parties, countries, and periods are more reliable than a topic's absolute share.
  • Use large enough cells. Most party series are best analysed by quarter or year, or pooled across parties.
  • Treat months without releases as missing, and use the coverage flags when comparing periods before and after December 2022.

The paper sets out these rules in full, along with the validation results.

Cite

Please cite the data paper (forthcoming) and ParlGov, the source of party identifiers and families. The earlier PARTYPRESS Database (Erfort, Stoetzer & Klüver 2023) is on the home page.
