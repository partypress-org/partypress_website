Before you analyse the data

  • Compare topics with policy-conditioned shares (as in the dashboard chart). How much non-policy content a release has is the least certain quantity in the data.
  • Compare rather than read levels. Differences across parties, countries, and periods are more reliable than a topic's absolute share.
  • Check that results do not depend on how sentences are counted. If a conclusion changes between expected and hard shares, or with the threshold for attending to an issue, report both.
  • Use cells large enough to be reliable. Most party series are best analysed by quarter or year, or pooled across parties. Treat months without releases as missing, and use the coverage flags when comparing periods before and after December 2022.
  • Correct when precision matters. When a claim rests on a level or a small difference, combine the predicted shares with a sample of reference codes.

Each rule is explained in the FAQ. The paper sets them out with the validation results.
